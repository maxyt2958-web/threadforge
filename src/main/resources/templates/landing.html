ThreadForge

Private, self-hosted workspace messaging — built for real-time channels, focused threads, direct calls, and rapid local file sharing.

  • Structured threads & channels — keep side discussions organized without drowning out the main room.
  • 1:1 voice & video calls — WebRTC calling directly from direct messages with ephemeral TURN relay support.
  • LAN P2P file sharing & rich Markdown — transfer large files over your local network, share code blocks, diagrams, and quick /poll votes.
  • Instant local search & SSO — embedded Lucene indexing with Keycloak OIDC authentication under your control.
